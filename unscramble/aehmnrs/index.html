What is on this page

These are all 199 words in our dictionary that the letters of SHERMAN can spell, using each letter no more times than it appears. They are grouped longest first, because in most word games length is what scores. The 101 everyday words are shown on amber tiles; the paler ones are real dictionary entries that most people would not reach for.

A different set of letters

This page is fixed to one rack. If you are holding something else, the word unscrambler does the same job for any letters you type, and the anagram solver finds only the words that use every letter exactly once.
