What is on this page

These are all 218 words in our dictionary that the letters of PATRONS can spell, using each letter no more times than it appears. They are grouped longest first, because in most word games length is what scores. The 126 everyday words are shown on amber tiles; the paler ones are real dictionary entries that most people would not reach for.
