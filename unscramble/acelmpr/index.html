What is on this page

These are all 181 words in our dictionary that the letters of CLAMPER can spell, using each letter no more times than it appears. They are grouped longest first, because in most word games length is what scores. The 97 everyday words are shown on amber tiles; the paler ones are real dictionary entries that most people would not reach for.
