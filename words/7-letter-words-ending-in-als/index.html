What is on this page

Every 7-letter word in our dictionary that end in ALS — 110 in all. They are split into the 56 words in ordinary use and the 54 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.
