What is on this page

Every 6-letter word in our dictionary that end in UT — 98 in all. They are split into the 12 words in ordinary use and the 86 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.
