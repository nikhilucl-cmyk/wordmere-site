What is on this page

Every 5-letter word in our dictionary that have L as the 4th letter — 916 in all. They are split into the 327 words in ordinary use and the 589 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.

Narrowing it down

A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The five-letter word solver takes a pattern like ???L? and narrows it further once you know a letter that is not in the answer, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
