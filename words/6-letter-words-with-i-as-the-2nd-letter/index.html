What is on this page

Every 6-letter word in our dictionary that have I as the 2nd letter — 2997 in all. A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The word finder takes a pattern like ?I???? and narrows it further once you know a letter that is not in the answer, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
