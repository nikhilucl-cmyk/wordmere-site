What is on this page

Every 7-letter word in our dictionary that end in OS — 240 in all. They are split into the 48 words in ordinary use and the 192 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.

Narrowing it down

A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The word finder combines this with the other letters you have already ruled in or out, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
