What is on this page

Every 5-letter word in our dictionary that end in PE — 52 in all. They are split into the 16 words in ordinary use and the 36 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.

Narrowing it down
