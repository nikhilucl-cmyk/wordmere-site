What is on this page

Every 5-letter word in our dictionary that end in LY — 185 in all. They are split into the 102 words in ordinary use and the 83 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.

Narrowing it down

A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The five-letter word solver combines this with the letters you have already ruled in or out, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
