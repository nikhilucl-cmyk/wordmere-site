What is on this page

Every 7-letter word in our dictionary that have R as the 6th letter — 3376 in all. A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The word finder takes a pattern like ?????R? and narrows it further once you know a letter that is not in the answer, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
