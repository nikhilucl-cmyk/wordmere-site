What is on this page

Every 5-letter word in our dictionary that start with T — 964 in all. They are split into the 335 words in ordinary use and the 629 that are valid in a word game but that almost nobody says. Most word lists do not make that distinction, which is why they are so hard to use: the answer you want is usually an everyday word.

Narrowing it down

A list this long is a starting point rather than an answer. The five-letter word solver combines this with the other letters you have already ruled in or out, and the word unscrambler works the other way round, from letters you are holding.
