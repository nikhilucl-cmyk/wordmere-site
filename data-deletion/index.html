This page explains how to have data associated with Wordmere deleted. Wordmere has no accounts — we never ask for your name, email address or a password — so there is no profile to close. What exists is described below, along with how to have it removed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email coolcontent1992@gmail.com with the subject line "Wordmere data deletion request".
  2. Tell us which device you play on (for example "Android phone" or "iPhone"). If you are able to, include your device's advertising ID — on Android it is in Settings → Privacy → Ads. This is optional, and it is the only thing that lets us identify advertising data as yours, since we hold no other identifier for you.
  3. We reply to confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also remove everything held on your device yourself, at any time and without contacting us, by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataOn requestRetention
Game progress and settings Held only on your device — uninstalling removes it immediately Until you uninstall or clear storage
Product analytics (PostHog, Firebase Analytics) Deleted Removed within 30 days of your request
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Deleted Removed within 30 days of your request
Advertising data (Google AdMob and its partners) Deletion requested on your behalf Held by those partners under their own policies; you can also reset or delete your advertising ID yourself at any time in your device settings
Purchase records (Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat) Kept Retained as long as required for tax, accounting and refund obligations. These are held by the store that processed your payment, not by us

Why so little

Wordmere runs on your device. Your level, coins, found words and settings never leave it. We hold no account, no email address and no name, which is why the advertising ID above is the only handle that exists for the small amount of data held off-device.
